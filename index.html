Why FPS Changes Sensitivity
Lower FPS (30 & 40) introduces high screen lag (~33.3ms), causing sluggish gyro drag. Our algorithm increases gyro ADS by +18% to +25% for 30/40 FPS to equalize recoil pull-down.
90 & 120 FPS High-Speed Gyro
At 90 & 120 FPS (8.3ms), gyro polling is hyper-responsive. We automatically apply high-frequency micro-dampening on 3x/4x/6x to eliminate reticle tremble during long-distance sprays.
Wrist vs Arm Pivot Technique
For 400% full gyro players: use your wrists for 180° snap rotations, and tilt your elbows/forearms down smoothly when pulling down 40-round sprays.
